## A first sound

A line that starts with a name and a colon is a **track**. Open a new file
with <Key command="shoreline.newFile" />, type the line below, then press
<Key command="shoreline.updatePatch" /> to play it.

```js
tone: $sine('c4')
```

While it plays, change `` `c4` `` to `` `e4` `` and press
<Key command="shoreline.updatePatch" /> again. The new version takes over at
the start of the next bar, and the sound never stops: the sine just moves to
the new note. That is the whole workflow: change something, update, listen.
When you've heard enough, press <Key command="shoreline.stop" /> to stop.

<Tip title="Help as you type">

Type `$` and autocomplete lists every module. Once one is in your code, hover
over its name, like `$sine` above, to read what it does, with an example. While
you type its arguments, the editor shows what each one is for. When you'd rather
browse, the **Modules** and **Globals** pages of this panel list everything.

</Tip>
