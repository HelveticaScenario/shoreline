## A first sound

A line that starts with a name and a colon is a **track**, and every track
gets its own strip in the mixer. Open a new file with <Key command="shoreline.newFile" />, type the line below, then
press <Key command="shoreline.updatePatch" /> to play it. When you've heard enough, press <Key command="shoreline.stop" /> to stop.

```js
tone: $sine('c4')
```

Change `c4` to `e4` and press <Key command="shoreline.updatePatch" /> again. The new version takes over
without a gap, and that is the whole workflow: change something, update,
listen.

<Tip title="Help as you type">

Type `$` and autocomplete lists every module, each with a short description.
Hover over a name to read its docs. When you'd rather browse, the **Modules**
and **Globals** pages of this panel list everything, with examples to copy.

</Tip>
