## A first sound

A line that starts with a name and a colon is a **track**. Open a new file
(<Key command="shoreline.newFile" />), type this and press <Key command="shoreline.updatePatch" /> to hear it.
<Key command="shoreline.stop" /> stops.

```js
tone: $sine('c4')
```

Change `c4` to `e4` and press <Key command="shoreline.updatePatch" /> again:
the new version takes over without a gap. That is the whole workflow.

<Tip title="Help as you type">

Autocomplete lists every module after `$`, each with a short description, and
hovering over a name shows its docs. The **Modules** and **Globals** pages of
this panel list everything, with examples to copy.

</Tip>
